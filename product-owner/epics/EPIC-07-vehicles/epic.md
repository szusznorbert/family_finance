# EPIC-07: Vehicles

## Goal

Track multiple household vehicles, their costs, required documents, and upcoming maintenance.

## Scope

- Vehicle details and archival.
- Vehicle expenses linked to canonical ledger entries.
- Fuel, insurance, tax, repairs, maintenance, inspection, toll, and rovinieta details.
- Private vehicle documents, expiry reminders, and per-vehicle summaries.

## Acceptance criteria

- A household can maintain separate records for multiple vehicles.
- Vehicle costs appear in vehicle history and household totals exactly once.
- Document expiry and maintenance reminders are visible to authorized members.
- Archived vehicles retain their financial and document history.
